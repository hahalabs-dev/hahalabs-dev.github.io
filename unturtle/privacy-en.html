Unturtle Privacy Policy

Effective: November 24, 2026

Unturtle is a Mac app made by HaHa Labs. This page explains what information Unturtle handles and what it does not. This policy applies to Unturtle only.

At a glance

Unturtle does not collect information. There is no account and no sign-in, and everything the app handles stays on your Mac.
  • There are no accounts. We do not ask for your name, email, or phone number.
  • Camera video is never saved and never leaves your Mac.
  • Your records and settings stay on your Mac.
  • The app does not connect to any server. There are no ads and no analytics tools.
  • There are no purchases.

Information that stays on your Mac

The following is used only on your Mac and is not sent anywhere.

InformationWhat it is used for
Camera videoThe camera turns on only while the screen is blurred, to check that you moved. No video or photos are saved. Only the position of your face and a number describing how much movement there was are computed on the spot and then discarded.
AirPods head-motion valuesTo check that you moved while you are wearing AirPods. They are not saved.
Whether the microphone is in useSo the screen does not blur during a call, the app only checks whether another app is using the microphone. It never listens to audio and does not request microphone access.
Sitting time and stretch recordsTo show your day. They are deleted when you delete the app.
SettingsTo remember choices such as the break interval.

Support email

If you email us through "Report a Problem", we receive your email address, your message, and the app version and macOS version that are pre-filled in the message. We use them only to reply and fix the problem, and delete them within one year after the matter is closed. The message is sent from your own mail app, not by Unturtle.

Sharing and processors

  • Because the app collects nothing, there is nothing to share and no processors.
  • We do not sell information or use it for advertising.

How to delete your information

  • Records and settings on your Mac: they are deleted when you delete the app.
  • Support email: ask us at the address below.

Children

Unturtle is not directed at children, and we do not collect children’s information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date. Before releasing anything that expands what we handle, such as features that work together with iPhone, we will update this page first and note it in the app’s update notes.
