Unturtle

Move to clear your screen

Sit too long and your Mac’s screen blurs for a moment. Move, and it clears.

In review for the Mac App Store · Coming soon

macOS 14 or later · Free · No account

The turtle is your body

A turtle lives in your menu bar. When you sit down, it starts walking. The longer you sit, the slower it goes, until it pulls its head in. Then your screen blurs for a moment.

  • Turn your head or roll your shoulders, the turtle stretches its neck back out, and the screen clears.
  • Every 30 or 60 minutes. You choose which.
  • You decide how long the movement takes, from 5 seconds to 3 minutes.

A heads-up before it blurs

A small heads-up appears one minute before the screen blurs, so nothing cuts you off mid-thought.

  • Start right away or snooze for 15 minutes.
  • It waits while you’re on a call. The screen doesn’t blur while your microphone is in use.
  • On a busy day, “Stop for Today” gives you the day off.

See your day at a glance

Click the turtle to see today as a single band.

  • Time spent sitting shows as a soft tint.
  • Each time you moved leaves a turtle footprint.
  • Your history stays on this Mac.

It only checks that you moved

The camera turns on only while the screen is blurred, and only to check that you moved.

  • Video is never saved and never leaves this Mac.
  • Click the eye on the blurred screen to see what the app looks at, as outlines only.
  • Wearing AirPods? Unturtle can notice your head movement through them too.
  • You can use Unturtle without the camera. In that case you press a button and stretch.

Pick your turtle’s color

Choose the color of the turtle you see when the screen blurs and in the one-minute heads-up. You can use the same color in the menu bar too.

FAQ

The screen blurred and I’m in a hurry

Click “Snooze 15 min”, or press and hold “Hold to Stop for Today” for two seconds. The blur is not a security lock.

What does the camera see?

It turns on only while the screen is blurred, to check that you moved. Video is never saved and never leaves your Mac. Click the eye at the bottom right of the blurred screen to see what the app looks at, as outlines only.

Can I use it without the camera?

Yes. Turn off “Check Movement with the Camera” in Settings → General. You then press a button and stretch.

Is there an iPhone app?

Features that work together with iPhone are on the way.
